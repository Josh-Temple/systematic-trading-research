# Horizontal Reaction Strategy v0.1

## Purpose

Mechanically test whether a recent horizontal support/resistance reaction remains economically useful after confirmation, executable entry, stop/target geometry, time stop, and transaction-cost-aware execution assumptions.

## Current boundary
