# Reaction-confirmation entry retains positive expectancy

## Statement

A mechanically defined reaction-confirmation entry around recent horizontal support/resistance zones may retain positive expectancy after entry confirmation, stop, target, time stop, and transaction-cost assumptions.

## Source basis

This statement is taken from the objective of the frozen Chat Branch Preregistration.

Source:
https://docs.google.com/document/d/1Uaq7dhaR2sJVXIp8yM4KqH12gvsZlnPhtRbxErA1pUA/edit

## Boundary

This hypothesis does not assert:

- profitability before testing,
- optimality of the v0.1 parameters,
- causality,
- broker-specific live-net profitability,
- that a horizontal touch by itself is tradable.

The v0.1 test specifically asks whether enough of the reaction remains after confirmation and executable risk management.
