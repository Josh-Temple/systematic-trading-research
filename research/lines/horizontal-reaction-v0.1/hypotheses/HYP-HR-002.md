# Unconditional horizontal-touch reaction

## Statement

Under the frozen horizontal-zone and approach-direction definitions, the mean direction-aware return from the first eligible touch to +15 completed minutes is positive in an unused sample even when events are included regardless of whether a later v0.1 confirmation occurs.

## Source basis

This distinct hypothesis is explicit in [Horizontal Reaction — Unconditional Touch Replication Preregistration — 2026H2 v1](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1kYvZ7tQ2m8ZynEjzVPZoy-k-cqsU6sWTqF4b_QeIkvo/edit), frozen 2026-09-13 before 2026H2 horizontal-touch outcome access.

## Boundary

This is a price-reaction hypothesis. It does not assert profitability, causality relative to a non-touch control, a touch-entry strategy, optimal holding time, confirmation delay, stop/target geometry, or live execution. The result applies to the frozen sample and definition only.
