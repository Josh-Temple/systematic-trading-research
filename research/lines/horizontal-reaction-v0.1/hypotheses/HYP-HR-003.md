# Confirmation-selection effect among unconditional horizontal touches

## Statement

Among all eligible first-touch events under the frozen horizontal-zone and approach-direction definitions, the original v0.1 confirmation rule selects events whose direction-aware touch-to-+15-completed-minute outcome is higher for `CONFIRMED` than `UNCONFIRMED` events (`PRIMARY_CONTRAST > 0`) in a separately frozen unused sample.

## Source basis

Explicitly preregistered in [Horizontal Reaction — Confirmation Selection Effect Preregistration — Post-H2 v1](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1GRa035N5FX3F59_wM2T11VFuv3aL85YZghmvaCeTJ0g/edit), frozen 2026-09-23.

## Boundary

This tests the selection effect of the existing confirmation rule. It does not optimize confirmation timing, thresholds, stops, targets, or trading performance. No outcomes have been computed or inspected for this preregistered test. Current state remains `WAITING_FOR_MATURITY`.
