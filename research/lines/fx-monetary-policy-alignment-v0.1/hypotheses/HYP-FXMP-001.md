# Price momentum × monetary-policy alignment

For the fixed currency universe AUD/CAD/CHF/EUR/GBP/JPY/NZD/USD:

> When the price-momentum-selected strongest currency has experienced a more restrictive three-calendar-month policy-rate change than the selected weakest currency, the following-month spot return of that selected pair is higher than when the relative policy-rate change points in the opposite direction.

The primary estimand proposed in SPEC-FXMP-001-v01 is:

`D = mean(y | ALIGNED) - mean(y | OPPOSED)`.

This is a hypothesis about incremental information / conditional heterogeneity.

It does **not** assert:

- that policy-rate changes are a complete measure of fundamentals;
- that a higher policy rate causes currency appreciation;
- that the relation is stable across regimes;
- that a positive historical association is executable or profitable after costs;
- that a null result means fundamentals do not matter.

No market evidence for HYP-FXMP-001 has been accessed or calculated.
